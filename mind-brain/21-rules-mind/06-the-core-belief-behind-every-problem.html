The Belief Behind
Almost Every Problem
You Have

The root beneath all limiting beliefs

02 — 06

There is one belief sitting underneath almost every problem you have ever had. And most people never even know it is there.

I am not enough.

Not smart enough. Not lovable enough. Not interesting, attractive, worthy enough.
Beneath the surface — it is almost always the same root.

03 — 06

People who feel they are not enough
spend their whole lives looking for more.

More money
More attention
More food
More validation
More of anything

No amount of external things can fix an internal belief.
The void stays until the belief changes.

04 — 06

This belief was not born in you.

You arrived knowing you were enough.

The learned belief

I must earn my worth. I need to be more, do more, prove more — only then will I be enough.

The original truth

Watch any baby. They do not doubt their worth. Enough-ness was your starting point.

It was learned. Which means it can be unlearned.

05 — 06

Write this down. Say it out loud.

Repeat it until your mind believes it again — because that is exactly how the mind works.

Your affirmation

I am enough.
I have always been enough.
I was born enough.

Say it. Write it. Repeat it.

06 — 06

When you transform
this belief —
you transform
everything.
