01 — 06

Your Mind Has Been Listening
to Everything You Have Ever
Said About Yourself

Every word. Every thought. Every quiet moment of self-doubt.

It believed all of it.

02 — 06

Your mind does not fact-check you

How it works

It does not stop and say "are you sure about that?"

It just accepts. Whatever you tell it, it takes as instruction. Then it works with everything it has to make that instruction real.

Every thought is either building you or dismantling you. There is no neutral.

03 — 06

Every rule is either working
for you or against you

Your mind does what it thinks you want.

It responds to pictures and words.

It loves what is familiar.

It learns by repetition.

04 — 06

The people who seem to
have it together

They do not have a different mind to you.

They have been giving their mind better instructions.

Better pictures. Better words. Better stories about who they are and what is possible.

05 — 06

You can start today

Not with a grand transformation. Not with a perfect mindset.

Just one better thought than the one you had yesterday.

Your mind learns by repetition. Repeat better things until they become the most familiar thing of all.
