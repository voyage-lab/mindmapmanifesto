The 3 Foundational
Rules of the Mind

Your mind is either working for you or against you

Working against you

When you don't know the rules — it runs on autopilot.

Working for you

When you understand it — you become the one giving instructions.

02 — 06

Rule 01

Your mind does exactly what it thinks you want — not what you actually want.

Keep saying these, and your mind goes — got it, delivering more:

"I am so stressed."

"I am so broke."

"I am so tired."

03 — 06

Rule 02

Your mind only responds to two things.

One

The pictures you make in your head

Two

The words you say to yourself

Change those two things and you literally start rewiring your reality.

04 — 06

Rule 03

Your mind loves what is familiar and hates what is unfamiliar.

This is why change feels so hard. But it is not —

not

Weakness

not

Laziness

just

Your mind doing its job — protecting what it already knows.

05 — 06

The good news?

How you work with the rules

You can make new things familiar.

You just have to repeat them until your mind believes you.

Not motivation. Not willpower. Repetition.
