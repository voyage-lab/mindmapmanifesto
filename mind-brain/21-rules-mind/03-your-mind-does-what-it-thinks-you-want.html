Your Mind Does
What It Thinks
You Want

It is not broken. It is taking your orders.

02 — 06

>

Every time you say it,
your mind hears it as an instruction.

>

"I always mess things up."

Understood →
>

"I can never stick to anything."

Understood →
>

"I am just not a confident person."

Understood →

It is not punishing you. It genuinely thinks that is what you want.

03 — 06

>

Your mind is a search engine.
It finds evidence for whatever you type in.

You type in

I am a failure

→

It finds

Every single failure. Proof everywhere.

You type in

I figure things out

→

It finds

Evidence of that instead. Every time.

It does not care if what you tell it is true or false. It just runs the search.

04 — 06

>

Notice the dramatic words.
Then find one that costs less.

Catastrophic

I am devastated

→

Manageable

I am disappointed

Catastrophic

I am a disaster

→

Manageable

I am still figuring it out

Catastrophic

I always fail

→

Manageable

This one did not work

Small word. Huge difference to your nervous system.

05 — 06

>

Try this today.

Your practice

Notice the dramatic negative words you use about yourself.

Just notice them. Then ask —

"Is there a word that means something less catastrophic?"

That is it. That is the whole practice.
