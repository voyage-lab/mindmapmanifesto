01 — 06

Why Change
Feels So Hard

Something inside you is actively fighting back

02 — 06

Your mind loves what is familiar.
It hates what is unfamiliar.

Familiar feels like

Safety. Home. Known. Comfortable — even when it hurts.

Unfamiliar feels like

Threat. Danger. Risk. Something to be avoided — even when it's growth.

That is how your brain was wired to survive.

03 — 06

This is why you go back.

not

Because you are weak

not

Because you do not want better

not

Because something is wrong with you

Because your mind is just doing its job — keeping you inside what it knows.

04 — 06

The secret is not willpower.

What doesn't work

Forcing yourself. White-knuckling it. Trying harder with the same approach.

What actually works

Repetition. Make the unfamiliar familiar. Do it enough — it becomes the new comfort zone.

Your mind stops seeing it as a threat. It becomes the new normal.

05 — 06

Stop asking why change is hard.

Ask a better question instead.

Your daily question

What am I making familiar today?

Answer it. Then do that thing.

06 — 06

You do not need
more willpower.

You need
more repetition.
