01 — 06

The Two Forces Controlling
Every Decision You Make

Every habit, every choice, every reaction — two forces

Force one

Pain — moving away from what hurts.

Force two

Pleasure — moving toward what feels good.

02 — 06

Your mind has one primary job

Keep you alive.

And it does that by constantly moving you away from pain and toward pleasure. Every single action you take traces back to this.

03 — 06

Here is what most people miss

The imbalance

The two forces are not equal.

Avoiding pain is a significantly stronger drive than seeking pleasure — because avoiding pain is how we stayed alive.

Your mind will always prioritise pain avoidance over moving toward something good.

04 — 06

This is why people stay stuck

The pain of change feels more immediate than the pleasure of a better life.

The pain of rejection feels more real than the pleasure of connection.

The pain of failure feels more certain than the pleasure of success.

05 — 06

Your mind is not being negative.
It is being protective.

There is a difference.

Once you understand this you stop fighting yourself — and start working with your mind instead of against it.

06 — 06

Tell it what you want.
Show it the pain of staying
is greater than
the pain of change.
