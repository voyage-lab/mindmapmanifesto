01 — 06

You Were
Born Enough

Not born with self-doubt. Not born with insecurity.

Not born feeling like you had to earn your place.

Not born performing for approval or shrinking to make others comfortable.

You were born enough.

02 — 06

Watch a baby

They do not wonder if they are worthy of love. They do not apologize for taking up space. They simply exist — with complete certainty that they matter.

That was you once. That is still you underneath everything that was layered on top.

03 — 06

At some point the world
started giving you feedback

A parent who was too busy.

A teacher who humiliated you.

A friend who left without explanation.

And your young mind landed on the cruelest possible answer — it must be me.

04 — 06

But that was not the truth

The real story

That was a conclusion made by a child with limited information and no other explanation available. You believed it so long it started to feel like fact.

It is not.

05 — 06

Your mind learns
by repetition

Write this down.
Say it out loud.
Repeat it until your mind accepts it.

06 — 06

I am enough.
I have always
been enough.
