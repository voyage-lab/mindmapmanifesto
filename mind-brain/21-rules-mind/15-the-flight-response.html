The Flight Response
And How It Is Secretly
Running Your Life

You do not have to physically run away to be in flight mode

Most people are fleeing without ever leaving the room.

02 — 06

When something feels
too overwhelming to confront

The nervous system finds another option — run.

Not always physically. But mentally. Emotionally. Get away from the threat. Get away from anything that feels like too much.

03 — 06

In adulthood, flight does not
look like running

Staying perpetually busy so you never have to sit with yourself.

Worrying constantly — catastrophizing the future so you don't have to feel the present.

Perfectionism — because if everything is perfect, nothing can hurt you.

Anxiety. Obsessive thinking. The inability to ever truly relax.

04 — 06

What makes flight so exhausting

The truth

You can never outrun yourself.

The thing you are fleeing is inside you.

No matter how busy you get, how much you achieve — it is still there. Waiting.

05 — 06

The antidote to flight
is not stillness for its own sake

It is safety.

Teaching your nervous system that it is safe to slow down.
Safe to feel. Safe to stop running.

06 — 06

All of it —
a nervous system
trying to outrun
something it never
fully escaped.
