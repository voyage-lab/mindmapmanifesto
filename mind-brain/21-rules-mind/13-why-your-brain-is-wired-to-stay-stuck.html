01 — 05

Why Your Brain Is
Wired to Stay Stuck

You know what you want. You know what you need to do.

So why do you not do it?

02 — 05

Your mind is not designed
for happiness

What it is designed for

Survival.

The single most effective survival strategy it has ever found is avoiding pain. Not seeking pleasure. Not chasing goals. Avoiding pain. That is the dominant program running in everything you do.

03 — 05

Think about the goals
you have not started

The risks you have not taken.

The conversations you keep postponing.

It is not laziness. Your brain ran a calculation — and decided the pain of trying and failing outweighed the pleasure of succeeding.

04 — 05

The part that stings

The pain your brain is protecting you from is often not even real.

It is a memory. A moment where trying led to embarrassment or rejection.

And your brain filed it away as — do not do that again.

05 — 05

Give your mind a
more compelling reason
to move.

Make the pain of staying
feel more real than
the pain of change.
