What You Want

The second great limiting belief

02 — 06

It is not available to me.

It sounds like realism. It feels like realism. But it is a belief — handed to you before you were old enough to question it.

I want love, but it never works out for me.
I want success, but I did not come from the right background.
I want to be healthy, but it runs in my family.

03 — 06

We learn what we live.
We live what we learn.

If the adults around you believed good things were hard to come by, you absorbed that.

If you grew up with very few choices — what to eat, what to wear, what to do — your mind concluded that options were not really yours to have.

Someone says you did an amazing job and you say —

That is not modesty. That is a belief running in the background.
