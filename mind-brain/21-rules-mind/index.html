  13. 13Why Your Brain Is Wired to Stay Stuck→You know what you want. You know what you need to do.
  14. 14The Fight Response→You probably think of fighting as something physical.
  15. 15The Flight Response→You do not have to physically run away to be in flight mode.
  16. 16The Freeze Response→Ever had a moment where you just could not move.
