## What This Reveals

### Insight #1 — Theta is the magnet (31 of 98)
Almost a third of the 98 states funnel into Theta. That's not a coincidence — it's the truth of the work. *Most human suffering is pattern-based, and patterns live below conscious thought.* Theta is where they get reached.

**Implication:** Piece #06 should be **the most-promoted, most-linked, most-optimized** of all 15. It is your homepage of the mind.

### Insight #2 — Alpha is the on-ramp (13 of 98)
Alpha pulls in everyone seeking calm, presence, gentleness. It's the **introvert's destination** — quiet, soft, available.

**Implication:** Piece #05 is your bridge content. Promote it heavily to anxiety/burnout audiences. From Alpha they will naturally drift into Theta.

### Insight #3 — Beta is the recognition piece (10 of 98)
People in Beta-overload don't know they are. They feel stuck, exhausted, angry, comparing. The Beta video is the **moment of recognition**: *"oh, that's me — I'm trapped in this state."*

**Implication:** This is your problem-aware audience's first stop. They become buyers when they realize they need to leave Beta.

### Insight #4 — Pride states stay analytical
Almost all 7 Pride cells route to *explainer* pieces: #01, #08, #09, #13. The proud need to *understand* before they will *try*.

**Implication:** Don't sell to Pride. Educate. Then they sell themselves.

### Insight #5 — The "Witness" layer always lands in #15
Most Witness-layer states (the meta-aware ones — *"why do I keep doing X"*) route to **#15** *Why Your Brain Is More Open to Change in Certain States.* Because that piece is **the** answer to the meta-question: *you can't think your way out.*

**Implication:** Piece #15 is the answer to every person who has tried everything and is asking *why.*

## How to Deploy This

### Phase 1 — Make Each of the 15 Findable
Each of the 15 already-shot videos becomes a **blog post / landing page** titled with the most universal phrasing:
- "What Theta Brain Waves Are and Why They Change Everything"
- "Why You're Trapped in Beta — and How to Escape"
- etc.

### Phase 2 — Build the 98 Funnel Posts
Each of the 98 cells becomes a **short blog post (300-600 words)** titled with **the literal question** from the table above.

The body of each:
1. Names the state ("Sound familiar? You're in a state called ___")
2. Explains why this is happening at a brain-wave level (1 paragraph)
3. **Links to the matching piece in the 15** — "*This is exactly what we cover in [Title of Piece]*"
4. CTA

Each piece is **5-minute writing** because the science is reusable. You're not writing 98 teachings. You're writing 98 invitations.

### Phase 3 — The Pillar/Cluster Map
- The 15 = **Pillar pages** (deep, comprehensive)
- The 98 = **Cluster pages** (specific, searchable, conversion-driven)
- Each cluster links to its pillar
- Each pillar links to relevant clusters

Google and AI both reward this structure exponentially.

### Phase 4 — Audit & Refine
After 90 days, check which cells drive the most traffic. **Double down on those archetypes.** Some niches will lean heavily Wrath-Crisis, others Sloth-Witness. Let the data tell you where your true audience lives.
