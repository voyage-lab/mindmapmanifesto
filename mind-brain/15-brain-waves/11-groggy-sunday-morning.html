01 — 06

That Groggy Sunday
Morning Feeling Is
Actually a Superpower

Most people just wait for it to pass. They should not.

02 — 06

What that state actually is

Your brain in theta — the same state used in hypnotherapy.

Half awake. Half asleep. Drifting. The analytical mind has not fully come back online yet. The part of you that judges and second-guesses is still slow and quiet. The deeper parts are unusually accessible.

03 — 06

Your brain makes connections
in that state that it
cannot make any other way.

When the analytical mind is running at full speed, it filters and organises. In theta, it makes connections it normally misses. Ideas arrive from nowhere. Problems that seemed stuck become clear.

04 — 06

Artists and writers have known
this for centuries.

Many of the most creative insights in history came in the half-awake morning state.

Edison reportedly napped in a chair holding steel balls — when he drifted into theta the balls dropped and woke him.

Your brain is doing something genuinely extraordinary in those moments.

05 — 06

What to do differently

Instead of rushing through it next time — stay in it a little longer. Notice what comes up. Notice what your mind drifts toward when nothing is demanding its attention. You might be surprised what is in there.

06 — 06

The slow Sunday morning
you have been
rushing past
is one of the most valuable
mental states you can be in.
