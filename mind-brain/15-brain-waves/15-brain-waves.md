## 02 — The 5 Brain Wave States Explained Simply

*Your brain is always producing electrical activity — it just runs at different speeds*

---

**State one — the fastest**
*Gamma.* Peak performance.

Heightened perception. *Flow state.* The feeling of being completely locked in. Your brain running at its highest and most connected.

---

**State two — your default**
*Beta.* Normal waking consciousness.

Active thinking. Analysis. Problem solving. **Most of us live here almost all day.** Useful — but most people never leave it long enough to recover.

---

This is where things get *interesting.*

**State three — Alpha**
Calm. Relaxed. Eyes closed. The gateway between your thinking mind and something deeper.

**State four — Theta**
Deep relaxation. Creativity. Daydreaming. This is where hypnosis lives — and where real change becomes possible.

---

**State five — the deepest**
*Delta.* Deep dreamless sleep.

The unconscious mind at rest. The body in full repair mode. *You visit this state every night.* You just have no memory of it.

---

Most people only know *two* of these states. **There is a whole** *spectrum in between.*

Learning to access the middle states is one of the most powerful things you can do for your mind.

## 03 — What Gamma Waves Are and When You Experience Them

*The fastest brain state — and the most electric*

---

You know *that feeling.*

When you are so locked into what you are doing that time disappears. Your thinking is sharp. Your perception is heightened. Everything feels connected. You are not trying. *You are just flowing.*

---

Gamma is the *fastest brain wave state* — 30 to 100 cycles per second.

Associated with *peak focus*, heightened awareness, and what psychologists call flow state. Your brain at its highest and most interconnected.

---

You have probably felt it *without knowing what it was.*

- Athletes feel it in the middle of a perfect game.
- Musicians feel it when a performance takes on a life of its own.
- Writers feel it when the words come faster than they can type.

---

**The key thing about gamma**
It is not something you can *force.* Trying to get into gamma usually pushes you further from it. It tends to emerge when you are **deeply engaged, highly skilled, and fully present.** The more you understand your brain states, the more you can create the conditions for it to happen.

---

Most people experience gamma in *short bursts* without ever realising **what it is.**

## 04 — What Beta Waves Are and Why Most of Us Are Stuck There

*An uncomfortable truth about modern life*

---

**What beta actually is**
Normal waking consciousness — *12 to 30 cycles per second.*

Active thinking. Analysis. Decision making. Problem solving. It is the state your brain *defaults to the moment your alarm goes off* in the morning — and rarely leaves.

---

Beta is *useful.* But most people never leave it.

**Why it is useful**
You need beta to function. To think, plan, analyse, navigate your day.

**Why it is a problem**
A brain that never slows down never recovers — never processes, never accesses the deeper states where healing happens.

---

What keeps us *locked in beta* all day.

- Constant stimulation — screens, notifications, alerts.
- Deadlines and decision fatigue that never fully resolve.
- The relentless inner monologue of a mind that never gets to rest.

---

**The goal**
Not to eliminate beta. *To stop being trapped there.* To learn how to shift states deliberately — so your brain gets the full range of what it is capable of. **Creativity. Recovery. Real insight.**

---

Most of us spend our entire waking day in *a single brain state.* **And it is slowly** *exhausting us.*

## 05 — What Alpha Waves Are and Why They Matter

*The brain state most people stumble into by accident — but almost nobody uses on purpose*

---

**What alpha actually is**
Calm, relaxed awareness — *8 to 12 cycles per second.*

Eyes closed. Body still. Mind quiet but *not asleep.* It is what your brain does naturally when you slow down and stop pushing — and it is far more powerful than most people realise.

---

You already go there *without trying.*

- Sitting in the sun and letting your thoughts drift.
- A long shower where ideas start flowing without effort.
- When you finally stop doing and just breathe.

---

Alpha is called *the gateway state.*

**What it sits between**
The busy analytical beta state — and the deep transformative theta state.

**Why that matters**
You cannot get to theta without passing through alpha. It is the bridge. The only way in.

---

**What opens in alpha**
The critical analytical part of your mind *starts to relax.* The inner voice that constantly judges and second-guesses begins to quiet. And in that quiet — **you become genuinely open.** To new ideas. To new feelings. To new beliefs about yourself.

---

This is why *meditation* works. Why *breathwork* works. Why *hypnosis* works. **They all lead** *to the same place.*

---

In theta, the *conscious mind steps aside.*

The deeper parts of your mind — the parts that hold your beliefs, your memories, your emotional responses, your ingrained patterns — *become accessible in a way they simply are not in normal waking consciousness.*

---

**Why theta matters for change**
Most patterns you want to change are stored *below the level of conscious thought.*

The fears. The limiting beliefs. The automatic reactions that seem to have a life of their own. **You cannot reach them by thinking harder.** You cannot reason your way to them. But in theta — *you can.*

---

**What the brain is doing in theta**
Not passive. *Deeply receptive.* Focused inward. And extraordinarily open to new information, new associations, and new ways of seeing itself. **This is not mystical. It is neuroscience.**

---

Theta is where *real change* becomes possible. **Not by force.** *By going deeper.*

## 07 — What Delta Waves Are and What Happens in Them

*The deepest brain state of all — and you visit it every single night*

---

**What delta actually is**
Deep dreamless sleep — *half a cycle to 4 cycles per second.*

The body in full repair mode. The conscious mind *completely offline.* The brain has turned off all non-essential activity so the body can do the work of healing, restoring, and rebuilding.

---

What your body does *in delta every night.*

- Growth hormone is released — the body rebuilds.
- The immune system does its most significant work.
- Memory consolidation — information moves from short-term to long-term storage.

---

The quality of your delta sleep affects *almost everything.*

**Poor delta sleep**
Poor recovery. Harder mood, focus, resilience. A brain running on less than it needs.

**Deep delta sleep**
Full recovery. A nervous system that can handle stress. A mind that shows up ready.

---

**The one thing to understand about delta**
You cannot access delta *consciously.* But you can create the conditions for it. **And that starts with understanding the states that lead you there.**

---

You visit delta *every single night.* **What happens there** *determines almost everything* about tomorrow.

## 15 — Why Your Brain Is More Open to Change in Certain States

*Some moments just feel different. More open. More clear. That is not random.*

---

**Beta — why change is so hard here**
In beta, your brain is *analytical, critical, and evaluative.*

It is constantly assessing, judging, and *protecting its existing model* of how things are and who you are. Useful for navigating daily life. But it is also exactly what makes change feel so hard.

---

You cannot think your way to a *new belief.*

Every time you try to install a new belief in beta, the critical mind is right there to challenge it. It will fact-check you against everything you have ever experienced. *And it will usually win.* Not because change is impossible — because you are trying in the wrong state.

---

**Alpha and theta — where change becomes possible**
The critical faculty *relaxes.* The gatekeeping quiets down.

The part of your mind that insists on keeping things as they are *loosens its grip.* And in that loosening — new information can land differently. New beliefs can take root. New associations can form between things that were previously locked in old painful patterns.

---

**The reframe**
This is not about bypassing your intelligence or your judgement. It is about accessing a part of your mind that your intelligence and judgement *normally drown out.* **The brain is not the same in every state. And knowing which state to be in for which kind of work might be the most useful thing you ever learn about yourself.**

---

The brain is not the same *in every state.* **Knowing which one to use** *changes everything.*
