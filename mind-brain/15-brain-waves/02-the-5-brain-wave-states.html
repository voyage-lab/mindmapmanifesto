01 — 06

The 5 Brain Wave
States Explained Simply

Your brain is always producing electrical activity — it just runs at different speeds

02 — 06

01

State one — the fastest

Gamma. Peak performance.

Heightened perception. Flow state. The feeling of being completely locked in. Your brain running at its highest and most connected.

03 — 06

02

State two — your default

Beta. Normal waking consciousness.

Active thinking. Analysis. Problem solving. Most of us live here almost all day. Useful — but most people never leave it long enough to recover.

04 — 06

This is where things get interesting.

State three — Alpha

Calm. Relaxed. Eyes closed. The gateway between your thinking mind and something deeper.

State four — Theta

Deep relaxation. Creativity. Daydreaming. This is where hypnosis lives — and where real change becomes possible.

05 — 06

05

State five — the deepest

Delta. Deep dreamless sleep.

The unconscious mind at rest. The body in full repair mode. You visit this state every night. You just have no memory of it.

06 — 06

Most people only know
two of these states.
There is a whole
spectrum in between.

Learning to access the middle states is one of the most powerful things you can do for your mind.
