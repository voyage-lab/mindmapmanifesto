## What This Reveals

### Insight #1 — "Not Enough" is the magnet (10 paths)
**Rule 06** pulls in more states than any other rule. That's not coincidence — it's the truth Mind Map Geo is built on. *Most human suffering descends from one root belief.*

**Implication:** This is your **hub piece**. Promote it hardest. Optimize it deepest. Link to it from every other rule. It's the bottom of the funnel for the soul.

### Insight #2 — Imagination > Logic is the second magnet (9 paths)
**Rule 04** is the *how does change happen?* answer. People come in through procrastination, weight loss, intimacy, creativity, purpose — and the answer is the same: **stop reasoning, start imagining.**

**Implication:** Rule 04 is your bridge content. It validates that they've been doing it wrong (logic) and shows the right doorway (imagination).

### Insight #3 — The Pain/Pleasure rule is universal (7 paths)
**Rule 12** is the *physics* of human behavior. Whenever someone asks "why do I do the thing I don't want to do" — the answer involves Rule 12.

### Insight #4 — Pride states stay on the surface
All 7 Pride cells route to *intellectual / framing* rules: 01, 02, 04, 06, 08, 15. The proud don't go straight to "you have childhood wounds." They need the *concept* first. The wound work comes later, once they've intellectually accepted the model.

**Implication:** Don't try to take Pride audiences into Rules 05, 11, 12, or 18 too fast. Move them through the framework first.

### Insight #5 — The "Crisis" layer goes to Fight/Flight/Freeze/Fawn
Almost every Layer-5 (Crisis) cell routes to **15, 16, 17, or 18** — the nervous system rules.

**Implication:** When someone is in crisis, they don't need belief work — they need *nervous system regulation* first. Crisis states must land in 15-18 or they bounce.

### Insight #6 — "Witness" layer questions are the deepest
"Why do I keep doing this?" questions route to **Rule 06, 07, 11, 19** — the deepest awareness rules. These are your most-aware audience. They're ready for the truth.

**Implication:** Witness-layer content can be the most direct, least sugar-coated. They've already done the dance with denial.

### Insight #7 — Patience archetype splits brain (4 nervous-system rules + 3 cognitive)
The Patience archetype splits cleanly between nervous-system work (Fawn, Flight, Loop-breaking) and cognitive work (Imagination, Better Things). This archetype is the most *recovery-oriented* of all 14.

## How to Deploy This

### Phase 1 — Make Each of the 21 Findable
Each rule becomes a **blog post / landing page / standalone video** titled with the search-friendly version of its concept:
- *"Why You Always Feel Like You're Not Enough (And How to Change It)"* — Rule 06
- *"The Real Reason You Can't Stick to Anything"* — Rule 09
- *"Why You Snap at the People You Love"* — Rule 14

### Phase 2 — Build the 98 Funnel Posts
Each of the 98 cells becomes a **300-600 word post** titled with the literal question from the table above.

Body structure:
1. **Name the experience** ("Sound familiar? You're in a state called ___")
2. **Validate it** (this is not your fault — it's how your mind works)
3. **Identify the rule** ("This is what Mind Map Geo calls Rule XX")
4. **Link to the full rule teaching**
5. **CTA**

Each piece is **5 minutes to write** because the teaching is reusable. You're not writing 98 teachings. You're writing 98 invitations.

### Phase 3 — Pillar / Cluster Architecture
- **The 21 rules = Pillar pages** (deep, comprehensive, evergreen)
- **The 98 cells = Cluster pages** (specific, searchable, conversion-driven)
- Each cluster links to its pillar
- Each pillar links to its 5-10 cluster pages

This is the structure Google AND every AI loves. It reads as authority.

### Phase 4 — Stack with the 15 Brain Waves
Where appropriate, **cross-link** between the two systems:
- Rule 04 (Imagination > Logic) → links to Theta video (#06 of Brain Waves)
- Rule 09 (Repetition) → links to Alpha + Theta videos
- Rule 18 (Loops) → links to Beta video (#04 of Brain Waves)
- Rule 13 (Wired Stuck) → links to "Why Brain Is Open to Change" (#15)

The 15 Brain Waves explains **WHY** the rules work at a neuroscience level.
The 21 Rules explains **HOW** to use the rules tactically.
Together = the complete operating system.
