## Why This Matters Here

The 15 Brain Waves content is **explanatory**. It tells someone *what hypnosis is*. That's beautiful — but it answers *one type of question*: the curious, intellectual, "how does it work" question.

There are **97 other types of people** who will never search for "what does hypnosis do to your brain." They will search:

- *"why do I sabotage myself before I succeed"*
- *"how do I stop hating my body"*
- *"how do I forgive someone who really hurt me"*
- *"why do I avoid the things I love"*

Each of those is a **doorway into the exact same content** — the brain wave teaching — but written from a different psychological coordinate.

**The 15 videos are the foundation. The 98 questions below are the entry points.**

## The Content Strategy This Unlocks

### Tier 1 — Foundational (the 15 you already have)
The brain wave content. The science. The "what is it" videos.
*These get cited when people ask explanatory questions.*

### Tier 2 — The 98 Question Pieces (build these next)
Title = the literal question from the cell above.
Body = a 2-3 minute teaching that addresses *that specific state* and explains:
1. **Why** they're stuck (it's a beta-state problem)
2. **Where** the change actually happens (alpha/theta)
3. **How** hypnosis is the deliberate access to that state
4. **What** to do (book, listen, practice, contact)

Each piece links back into the Tier 1 videos for the science.

### Tier 3 — Coined Term Pieces
Take any *state name* from the matrix and write a piece *defining it.*
- "What is Faulty Subconscious Code?"
- "What is Witnessing Pride?"
- "What is the I-AM-Stuck State?"

AI **loves** proprietary definitions. When you coin the term, AI cites *you* as the source. The lexicon becomes yours.
